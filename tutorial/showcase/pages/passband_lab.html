Photometry is an integral over the spectrum

Drag the orange filter across a SPICE spectrum, or pull its edges to change the width. The page integrates the photon-counting AB magnitude live. For the Strömgren v, b and y presets it also shows the value SPICE itself computed, so you can check the two agree.

The spectra are disc-integrated syntheses from the TPayne-spice-harps-interp v1.2.0 emulator over its full 3790–6900 Å range (15,000 points), for a K dwarf, the Sun and an A/F star at 10 pc. Only passbands that fit entirely inside that range give meaningful magnitudes, which is why Johnson B and V are not offered.
